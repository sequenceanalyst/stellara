Sponsorships are agreements between Trainers and companies or organizations that provide support, resources and opportunities in exchange for promoting their products, services or interests. Sponsorships are especially common among professional Trainers who participate in the Stellara League, although anyone can receive a sponsorship depending on their accomplishments, reputation and activities.

Sponsorships are primarily narrative opportunities. They may provide access to events, facilities, equipment, professional connections and occasional rewards, but they do not grant passive EXP, automatic progression benefits or other direct mechanical advantages.

Sponsorship Tiers

Sponsorships are divided into four tiers, which determine the prestige of the sponsoring organization, the opportunities it can provide and the responsibilities expected of the sponsored individual.

Local Sponsorships: Agreements with small businesses and organizations. These generally provide modest support, community recognition and opportunities to participate in local events or promotional activities. Players may establish Local Sponsorships independently through roleplay.

Regional Sponsorships: Agreements with established companies and organizations operating throughout Stellara. These may provide greater publicity, travel opportunities, access to events and professional connections. Players may establish Regional Sponsorships independently through roleplay.

Major Sponsorships: Agreements with prominent corporations and organizations that provide substantial publicity, prestigious opportunities and access to specialized resources. These sponsorships are generally reserved for individuals with considerable accomplishments or recognition and require staff approval.

Premier Sponsorships: Agreements with exceptionally prestigious or internationally recognized organizations. These represent some of the most exclusive professional partnerships available and may provide extraordinary narrative opportunities and recognition. Premier Sponsorships require staff approval.

Primary and Secondary Sponsorships

Each Trainer may have TWO primary sponsorships at a time. Their primary sponsors represent their main professional partnership and provides the full narrative benefits and opportunities appropriate to their sponsorship tier.

Trainers may also establish secondary sponsorships with other companies or organizations. Secondary sponsorships are smaller arrangements that may provide occasional promotional opportunities, temporary resources or other minor benefits. They do not provide the same level of support or privileges as a primary sponsorship.

There is no fixed limit on secondary sponsorships, although the number and nature of these agreements must remain reasonable. Sponsorship agreements should not conflict with one another unless those conflicts are intentionally explored through roleplay.

Obtaining and Maintaining Sponsorships

Sponsorships are earned through a Trainer's accomplishments, reputation, professional activities and character development. There are no fixed Trainer Tier requirements, as sponsorships are not limited to competitive battlers.

Local and Regional Sponsorships may be created and roleplayed independently. Major and Premier Sponsorships require staff approval to ensure that the character's accomplishments and reputation justify the agreement.

Sponsored Trainers are expected to reasonably fulfill the responsibilities associated with their agreements. These may include public appearances, promotional activities, participation in sponsored events or representing their sponsors professionally.

There are no mandatory monthly activity requirements. However, repeatedly neglecting sponsorship obligations or acting against a sponsor's interests may result in narrative consequences, including the termination of the agreement.

Sponsorships may be changed or ended through roleplay. A Trainer must end their existing primary sponsorship before establishing another.

Any EXP, Pokémon or permanent gameplay-related rewards associated with sponsored activities must follow the appropriate rules for Missions, catching, the Pokémart or other relevant systems.
