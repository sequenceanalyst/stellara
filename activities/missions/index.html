Missions are adventures and assignments that Trainers can undertake to earn EXP, obtain valuable items, encounter rare Pokémon and develop their characters. Missions are ranked from one to six stars, with higher-ranked missions presenting greater challenges, dangers and rewards.

Missions can involve anything from helping local residents and exploring dangerous environments to confronting powerful Pokémon, investigating criminal organizations and responding to regional emergencies.

Available Missions

Choose a mission to read its full briefing. You can search by keyword, or narrow the list by difficulty, type, issuer and whether a mission can be repeated or done alone.

Mission Difficulty

★ 1-Star — Routine

Basic assignments such as deliveries, locating missing Pokémon, assisting residents and investigating minor disturbances. These missions are generally safe and suitable for inexperienced Trainers.

★★ 2-Star — Moderate

More demanding assignments involving troublesome wild Pokémon, hazardous environments, minor criminal activity or rescue operations. Injuries are possible, but the dangers are generally manageable with proper preparation.

★★★ 3-Star — Challenging

Difficult expeditions, dangerous Pokémon encounters, organized criminal activity and complex investigations. These missions present considerable risks, and Trainers who underestimate them may suffer serious injuries or significant setbacks.

★★★★ 4-Star — Severe

Highly dangerous assignments involving exceptionally powerful Pokémon, major emergencies, dangerous criminals or extreme environments. Serious injuries and potentially fatal situations are genuine possibilities, even for accomplished Trainers.

★★★★★ 5-Star — Extreme

Extraordinarily dangerous missions involving overwhelming adversaries, catastrophic threats and situations where even exceptional Trainers may struggle to survive. Death, permanent injuries and major narrative consequences are genuine possibilities. These missions must be moderated by staff.

★★★★★★ 6-Star — Catastrophic

The most dangerous assignments available in Stellara, reserved for Trainers with extraordinary abilities and experience. These missions may involve threats capable of devastating entire cities or regions, with overwhelming enemies and exceptionally difficult objectives. Survival is never guaranteed, and failure may carry devastating consequences. Six-star missions are exceptionally rare and must be moderated by staff.

Mission Requirements

Organizations issuing missions evaluate a Trainer's qualifications before assigning dangerous work. Trainers must meet the minimum requirements for a mission before participating, regardless of whether they are accompanied by more experienced Trainers.

Undertaking Missions

One- through four-star missions may be roleplayed independently without staff moderation. Players are responsible for portraying the mission's events, NPCs and challenges while remaining consistent with the mission's objectives and difficulty.

Five- and six-star missions must be moderated by staff due to their difficulty, danger and potential narrative consequences.

Players may begin ONE new mission per week and participate in a maximum of TWO active missions at a time. The weekly limit resets every Monday.

Missions may be completed alone or with other players. However, players may complete a maximum of ONE solo mission per calendar month. There are no fixed participant limits for multiplayer missions, although the number of participants should remain reasonable for the mission's objectives and circumstances.

Players may also propose their own missions for staff approval. Approved missions may be added to the available mission listings.

Mission Rewards

Missions award the standard EXP earned through roleplay, along with an additional completion bonus determined by their difficulty.

These bonuses are awarded in addition to the standard 5 EXP per qualifying post and 50 EXP for completing a qualifying thread with at least 10 posts per participant.

Missions may also award Pokémon encounters, rare items, equipment and other rewards depending on their objectives and difficulty. Available rewards will be specified in the mission listing.

Every eligible participant receives the advertised EXP reward upon successful completion. Certain items and other special rewards may have additional restrictions.

Completing Missions

Once a mission has been completed, players must submit it for approval in the Mod Request section of the Discord.

Staff will verify that the mission's objectives were completed, participation requirements were met and the rewards are appropriate. Staff are not required to review every post in detail unless there are concerns about the mission's completion or conduct.

Once approved, participants may claim their rewards and update their records accordingly.

Missions should involve meaningful roleplay and reasonably reflect their assigned difficulty. Players should not artificially divide missions into multiple threads or otherwise exploit the system to earn additional rewards.
