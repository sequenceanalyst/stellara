Overview

The Stellara Trials are a series of challenges designed to test the strength, intelligence, perseverance and capabilities of Trainers and their Pokemon. Unlike the Pokemon League, which primarily evaluates a Trainer's ability to battle, the Stellara Trials challenge both Trainers and Pokemon by placing them in dangerous situations. Trainers may be required to overcome powerful opponents, survive hazardous environments and make difficult decisions that directly affect themselves and their Pokemon.

The Stellara Trials are overseen by the Stellara Trial Association, which is responsible for evaluating applicants and organizing the challenges. Trainers must have at least three GREAT Tier Pokemon to participate. There are eight Trials divided into four stages, with each stage containing two Trials. Trainers may challenge either Trial within their current stage in any order, but must complete both before advancing to the next stage.

Stages and Trials

The Trials and their respective stages are listed below:

  • Stage I: Trial of Endurance and Trial of Ingenuity.
  • Stage II: Trial of Courage and Trial of Resolve.
  • Stage III: Trial of Trust and Trial of Leadership.
  • Stage IV: Trial of Adaptation and Trial of Sacrifice.

Eligibility

Each stage becomes progressively more difficult and requires Trainers to have stronger Pokemon. The minimum requirements for participating in each stage are listed below:

  • Stage I: At least three GREAT Tier Pokemon.
  • Stage II: At least three ULTRA Tier Pokemon.
  • Stage III: At least three ELITE Tier Pokemon.
  • Stage IV: At least three CHAMPION Tier Pokemon.
  • Legend's Trial: A full team of six Pokemon, including at least one MASTER Tier Pokemon and three additional CHAMPION Tier Pokemon. The remaining two Pokemon must be at least ELITE Tier.

Rules and Risks

Unlike conventional Pokemon battles, Trainers are not protected from opposing Pokemon during the Trials. Trainers may be directly attacked, injured or otherwise placed in danger. Injuries, exhaustion and other consequences should be acknowledged throughout the roleplay. Trainers are expected to approach these situations realistically and cannot simply ignore attacks or other threats directed toward them.

Although the Trials can result in serious injuries, permanent character death or the death of a Pokemon requires the affected player's consent. Participating in the Stellara Trials does not automatically constitute consent to these consequences.

The Trials are staff-moderated events. While Trainers are expected to overcome difficult challenges, successfully completing a Trial does not necessarily require winning every battle. Staff will evaluate the Trainer's overall performance, including their decision-making, creativity, cooperation with their Pokemon and willingness to acknowledge the consequences of their actions. Players who roleplay their challenges fairly and meaningfully will generally have a greater likelihood of passing.

Each Trial has its own objectives, rules and challenges, which will be communicated to participants before the Trial begins. However, certain obstacles and unexpected developments may remain undisclosed until they occur. Staff are expected to evaluate participants fairly and provide a brief explanation when a Trainer fails a Trial.

Due to the amount of moderation involved, Trials are scheduled according to staff availability. Staff may limit the number of active Trials, establish scheduling requirements or temporarily postpone new challenges when necessary. Trainers are encouraged to communicate with staff when they are ready to participate.

Trial Crests

Successfully completing a Trial awards the Trainer its corresponding Trial Crest, which serves as official recognition of their accomplishment. Trainers must collect all eight Trial Crests before they can challenge the Legend's Trial.

Failing a Trial does not prevent a Trainer from attempting it again. However, Trainers must wait one month OOC before retrying a failed Trial. Trainers may not advance to the next stage until both Trials in their current stage have been completed.

Select a Crest to see its Trial's stage and requirement.

Legend's Trial

After completing all eight Trials, Trainers become eligible to challenge the Legend's Trial, provided they meet its Pokemon requirements. This is the final and most difficult challenge, intended to push even exceptionally powerful Trainers and their Pokemon to their limits. The Legend's Trial requires a full team of six Pokemon and features opponents capable of challenging Pokemon belonging to regional Champions and Pokemon Masters.

Paragon

Trainers who successfully complete the Legend's Trial earn the title of Paragon. Paragons are recognized as some of the most accomplished Trainers in Stellara, with prestige comparable to that of a regional Champion. Unlike Champions, whose accomplishments primarily demonstrate their battling capabilities, Paragons have proven themselves capable of overcoming extraordinary dangers alongside their Pokemon.
