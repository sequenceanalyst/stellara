The Stellara Pokémon League has a reputation for being one of the most difficult Pokémon Leagues to complete. Much of this comes from the sheer skill and power of its Gym Leaders, as well as the challenges that Trainers must overcome to earn their badges. While Gym challenges are generally settled through Pokémon battles, they aren't always ordinary battles. Each Gym has its own rules, challenges and obstacles designed to test a Trainer's strength, strategy and ability to adapt.

There are eighteen main Gyms in Stellara, each specializing in a different Pokémon type and located within one of the region's settlements. Although other Gyms exist throughout Stellara, these eighteen are officially recognized as part of the main League circuit. Trainers only need to obtain eight Gym Badges to qualify for the Champion Cup, and Gyms may be challenged in any order.

Gym Challenges

Gym challenges become progressively more difficult depending on how many badges a Trainer has earned. Gym Leaders will adjust their Pokémon and strategies to provide an appropriate challenge, preventing experienced Trainers from simply overwhelming weaker teams. The following guidelines describe the average Pokémon Tier a Gym Leader will use based on the challenger's current badge count.

BadgesPokémon Tier
0–1Rookie
2–3Experienced
4–5Great
6–7Ultra
8+Elite

These Tiers are guidelines rather than strict limitations. Gym Leaders may use stronger Pokémon when necessary to provide a fair challenge, particularly if the challenger has Pokémon that are significantly stronger than the listed Tier. However, Gym Leaders should not deliberately create impossible battles or overwhelm challengers with unreasonable advantages.

To initiate a Gym challenge, post in the Gym's designated board detailing your character's arrival and intention to challenge the Gym Leader. Be sure to read the Gym's rules beforehand, as individual Gyms may have different battle formats, obstacles and other requirements. These must be communicated before the challenge begins and must follow Stellara's Battle Rules.

If a Trainer successfully completes a Gym challenge, they will receive that Gym's badge. If they lose or forfeit, they must wait one week before challenging the same Gym again. This cooldown does not prevent Trainers from challenging other Gyms in the meantime.

Becoming a Gym Leader

Stellara's Gym Leaders are among the region's competitive elite. Their responsibility is to challenge Trainers, evaluate their skills and promote a competitive but healthy culture of Pokémon battling. Members may apply to become Gym Leaders either when creating their characters or later during roleplay.

To become a Gym Leader, members must submit an application identifying the Gym they wish to lead, explaining their character's qualifications and describing how they intend to make their Gym challenging and interesting. Applications are reviewed by staff, who will determine whether the character is suitable for the position.

Gym Leaders are expected to be fair, respectful and reasonably active. They must maintain an information thread describing their Gym, its battle format, rules and any rewards offered to challengers. Gym Leaders may customize their Gym's appearance, challenges and overall identity, but the Gym must remain within its assigned settlement. Any special rules or additional rewards must be approved by staff.

Gym Leaders are not required to prepare separate teams for every possible badge count. Instead, they may maintain a roster of Pokémon and adjust their team compositions according to the challenger's progression. All Pokémon and battle formats must follow the official rules.

Gym Leaders may also establish a reasonable limit on how many challenges they handle simultaneously. They are not expected to accept every challenge immediately, but they should communicate their availability and avoid unnecessarily delaying challengers.

If a Gym does not have an active player-controlled Gym Leader, staff may arrange for an NPC Gym Leader to oversee its challenges. Staff may also temporarily substitute an NPC when a player-controlled Gym Leader is unavailable for an extended period. This ensures that Trainers can continue progressing through the League without being dependent on any particular member's activity.

Gym Leader positions may be reassigned if a member becomes consistently inactive or is otherwise unable to fulfill the responsibilities of the position.

The Champion Cup

Unlike many other regions, Stellara does not have an Elite Four. Instead, Trainers who obtain at least eight official Gym Badges qualify for the Champion Cup, a prestigious region-wide tournament where qualified Trainers and Gym Leaders compete for the opportunity to challenge the reigning Champion.

The Champion Cup follows a single-elimination format, with the tournament winner earning the right to challenge the Champion in a separate Championship match. Winning the tournament does not automatically grant the title of Champion; the challenger must defeat the reigning Champion to claim it.

Although the Champion Cup is traditionally held annually within Stellara, tournaments will be organized as major server events whenever participation and activity make them practical. Additional tournament rules and details will be announced when a Champion Cup is scheduled.
