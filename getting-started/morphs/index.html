Morphs are individuals who possess Pokémon DNA, either naturally or through artificial genetic modification. Natural Morphs have existed in Stellara for thousands of years, although their exact origins remain unknown. One theory suggests that ancient civilizations who revered Pokémon were blessed with some of their characteristics and abilities.

Morphs possess physical characteristics associated with the Pokémon whose DNA they carry. Some may also exhibit minor abilities related to their Pokémon heritage. However, these abilities are considerably weaker than those of actual Pokémon and are primarily intended for roleplay rather than combat.

Morph Characteristics and Abilities

  • Morphs must possess noticeable physical characteristics of their Pokémon species, such as tails, ears, horns, fur, scales or other distinctive features. Minor differences such as unusual eye colors are not sufficient on their own.
  • Morphs may only possess DNA from one Pokémon species. Legendary Pokémon, Mythical Pokémon and other specially restricted species cannot be used without explicit staff approval.
  • Morphs may possess minor physical traits or heightened senses appropriate for their species. However, they are not inherently stronger, faster or more durable than ordinary humans.
  • Morphs may possess one minor supernatural ability related to their Pokémon species, such as limited telekinesis, producing a small flame or sensing nearby emotions. This ability must be described during character creation and approved by staff.
  • Morphs cannot use conventional Pokémon moves or Abilities, transform into Pokémon, or utilize special battle mechanics such as Mega Evolution, Dynamax or Terastallization.
  • Morph abilities cannot provide significant combat advantages, replicate powerful Pokémon attacks, automatically incapacitate opponents or bypass established roleplay restrictions.
  • Morphs remain vulnerable to injuries, exhaustion and other limitations that affect ordinary humans. They cannot reasonably battle on equal footing with Pokémon solely because of their genetic heritage.

Natural Morphs

Natural Morphs are born with Pokémon DNA inherited through their family. At least one parent must be a Morph, although their appearance and abilities may differ between generations.

Natural Morphs are not the result of reproduction between humans and Pokémon. They have existed as part of human society for generations and may have the same backgrounds, occupations and lifestyles as anyone else.

Team Crux has taken a particular interest in natural Morphs because of their genetic characteristics and frequently seeks them out for research. However, playing a natural Morph does not automatically require involvement in a Team Crux storyline.

Artificial Morphs

Artificial Morphs were originally ordinary humans who acquired Pokémon DNA through scientific experimentation and genetic modification.

Team Crux is currently the only organization in Stellara known to possess the technology necessary to create artificial Morphs. As a result, artificial Morphs generally have some connection to Team Crux, whether as members, former subjects or individuals who escaped its facilities.

Artificial Morphs who defect from Team Crux may attract the organization's attention. However, any significant pursuit, capture or other narrative consequences involving player characters must follow Stellara's established roleplay rules and be coordinated with the affected players.

Additional Restrictions

Morph abilities must remain consistent with what was approved during character creation. Players cannot introduce additional powers or increase their abilities beyond their established limitations without staff approval.

Morphs do not possess Pokémon Tiers, type-based immunities or the combat capabilities of their corresponding Pokémon species. Their abilities are intended to provide additional characterization and roleplay opportunities rather than a competitive advantage.

Staff may require adjustments to Morph concepts or abilities that are excessively powerful, difficult to balance or inconsistent with these rules.
