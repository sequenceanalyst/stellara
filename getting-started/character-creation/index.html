Players may create their character sheets using Discord, Google Docs; etc. Players may use their own custom templates. However, all character sheets must include the required information listed below. If your character sheet is hosted elsewhere, you must provide a link to it.

Character Information

Name
Your character's name.
Age
Your character's age. The minimum age is 18, with no exceptions.
Gender
Your character's gender.
Character Type
Whether your character is a Human or Morph. If they are a Morph, specify their Pokémon species and whether they are a Natural or Artificial Morph.
Orientation
Your character's sexual orientation.
Trainer Tier
Your character's starting Trainer Tier. Characters may begin at any Tier up to Great, provided their background reasonably supports their experience and accomplishments.
Affiliation Optional
Any organizations or factions your character belongs to, if applicable. This field is optional.
Face Claim Optional
Your character's face claim, if applicable. This field is optional.
Height
Your character's height.
Body Type
Your character's body type.
Hair Color
Your character's hair color.
Eye Color
Your character's eye color.
Skin Color
Your character's skin color.
About
A freeform section describing your character's personality, background, experiences or anything else you consider important. This section must contain at least 150 words. Morph characters should also describe their Pokémon characteristics and any minor abilities they possess, following Stellara's Morph Rules.
Starting Pokémon
List all Pokémon your character begins with using the established Pokémon template provided below. Each Pokémon must have a complete entry containing all required information. Starting teams must follow Stellara's Starter Pokémon Rules, including the 10-point allocation system and maximum of six Pokémon.

Pokémon Template

Name
Your Pokémon's name or nickname, if applicable.
Species
Your Pokémon's species.
Tier
Your Pokémon's starting Tier.
Type
Your Pokémon's typing.
Ability
Your Pokémon's Ability.
Gender
Your Pokémon's gender, including genderless Pokémon.
Height
Your Pokémon's height. This may differ from its official Pokédex entry.
Weight
Your Pokémon's weight. This may differ from its official Pokédex entry.
Move List
All moves your Pokémon currently knows.
Personality
A brief description of your Pokémon's personality, temperament and other notable characteristics.
Acquisition
How your Pokémon was obtained, such as through catching, adoption, trading or a Mission. For starting Pokémon, briefly explain how your character obtained them.

Each starting Pokémon must have its own completed entry. These entries should also be maintained in your character's Personal Computer (PC) as your Pokémon progress.
