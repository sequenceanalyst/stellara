The Personal Computer (PC) is where players keep records of their Pokémon, items and other important possessions. Every player must maintain an organized and regularly updated PC to ensure that their Pokémon and inventory information can be easily verified.

Players may use their own PC templates and customize their appearance however they wish, provided that all required information is included and clearly accessible.

Pokémon Information

Every Pokémon owned by a character must have an individual entry containing the following information:

  • Name: Your Pokémon's name or nickname, if applicable.
  • Species: Your Pokémon's species.
  • Tier: Your Pokémon's current Tier.
  • Type: Your Pokémon's typing, including any changes resulting from evolution.
  • Ability: Your Pokémon's current Ability.
  • Held Item: Any item your Pokémon is currently holding, if applicable.
  • Gender: Your Pokémon's gender, including genderless Pokémon.
  • Height: Your Pokémon's height. This may differ from its official Pokédex entry.
  • Weight: Your Pokémon's weight. This may differ from its official Pokédex entry.
  • Move List: All moves your Pokémon currently knows.
  • Personality: A brief description of your Pokémon's personality, temperament and other notable characteristics.
  • Acquisition: How your Pokémon was obtained, such as through catching, adoption, trading or a Mission. Include a link to the relevant thread or transaction when applicable.

Players may include additional information such as their Pokémon's history, appearance, relationships or other details, but these are optional.

Item Inventory

Players must also maintain an inventory of the items their characters possess. Each item should be listed with its name and quantity, where applicable.

This includes Pokéballs, healing items, evolutionary items, held items, special battle equipment and other possessions obtained through Stellara's systems.

Items that are automatically available under Stellara's rules, such as ordinary Pokéballs used for catching, do not need to be individually tracked unless otherwise specified.

Maintaining Your PC

Players are responsible for keeping their PCs accurate and up to date. Changes such as catching or obtaining Pokémon, evolution, Tier advancement, learning or replacing moves, and acquiring or using limited items must be reflected in the PC.

Temporary conditions such as ordinary battle injuries, status ailments or exhaustion do not need to be recorded unless they have lasting narrative consequences.

Information not recorded in your PC cannot be assumed to be available to your character. Players may be required to correct inconsistencies, and actions involving unrecorded Pokémon, moves, Abilities or items may be voided if necessary.

Staff may review PCs to verify information or resolve disputes. Repeated or deliberate misuse of the PC system may result in additional moderation action.
