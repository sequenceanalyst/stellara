Stellara uses a point-buy system for starting Pokémon. Trainers begin with TEN POINTS, which they may spend to create a starting team of up to six Pokémon. Each Pokémon costs a certain number of points depending on its Tier.

  • Beginner: 1 Point
  • Rookie: 2 Points
  • Experienced: 3 Points
  • Great: 4 Points

Any unspent points are converted into EXP at a rate of 25 XP per point.

Trainers may choose their starting Pokémon freely, provided they follow Stellara's established species and acquisition restrictions. Legendary Pokémon, Mythical Pokémon, Ultra Beasts, Paradox Pokémon and other restricted species cannot ordinarily be selected as starters.

Each starting Pokémon may begin with one Technical Machine (TM) or Egg Move, provided it can legitimately learn that move and does not exceed its available move slots. Starting Pokémon cannot be Shiny or possess Hidden Abilities.
