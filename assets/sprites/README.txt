Put sprite pictures for the Pokémon listings here.

Save one PNG or WebP per Pokémon (square, about 256 to 512 pixels, under about 100 KB), with a lowercase name and no spaces, such as eevee-biscuit.png.
Then add  sprite: "eevee-biscuit.png"  to that Pokémon in assets/listings-data.js.
Only use pictures you have the right to use.
