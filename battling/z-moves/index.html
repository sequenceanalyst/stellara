Z-Moves are exceptionally powerful techniques that allow Pokémon to unleash extraordinary attacks or enhance the effects of their existing moves. Although originally discovered in Alola, knowledge of Z-Moves has since spread to Stellara.

To use Z-Moves, Trainers must possess a Z-Ring and the appropriate Z-Crystal, both of which can be purchased from the Pokémart. Trainers must also possess at least THREE EXPERIENCED-TIER POKÉMON to unlock Z-Moves.

There are three categories of Z-Moves:

  • Type-Specific Z-Moves: Powerful attacks that require a compatible move and the corresponding Z-Crystal.
  • Z-Status Moves: Status moves enhanced by Z-Power, granting additional effects appropriate to the original move.
  • Exclusive Z-Moves: Special techniques available only to specific Pokémon species with the appropriate Z-Crystal.

Z-Moves may generally be used ONCE PER THREAD, although this restriction may be adjusted if everyone involved agrees.

Z-Move Damage

Damaging Z-Moves increase the original move's damage by TWO CATEGORIES, with a minimum of HEAVY and a maximum of DEVASTATING. Type effectiveness applies normally according to Stellara's existing Battle Rules.

Although Z-Moves are exceptionally powerful, they do not guarantee a knockout or victory. Their effectiveness still depends on the user's Tier, the target's capabilities, defenses and current condition. Z-Moves do not allow Pokémon to bypass established Tier differences.

Z-Moves are particularly difficult to defend against, but opponents must still be given reasonable opportunities to respond. Using a Z-Move also requires considerable energy, and Pokémon may experience noticeable exhaustion afterward.

Z-Status Moves do not follow the damaging Z-Move rules. Instead, they enhance the effects of their original status moves as appropriate.

Custom Z-Moves are permitted with staff approval. Players wishing to create one must submit its name, eligible Pokémon species and a short description through their Pokémart thread. Custom Z-Moves cannot introduce unreasonable advantages or effects that contradict Stellara's Battle Rules. Once approved, they become available to other eligible players.
