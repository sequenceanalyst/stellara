Dynamax is a temporary transformation that drastically increases a Pokémon's size, enhances its physical capabilities and transforms its moves into powerful Max Moves.

Although Dynamax originated in Galar, Galar Particles have spread to Stellara, resulting in the formation of numerous Power Spots throughout the region.

Dynamax requires a Dynamax Band and access to a Power Spot. Dynamax Bands can be purchased from the Pokémart, while Power Spots are found in stadiums, Gyms and certain wilderness locations.

Dynamaxing in cities or towns is generally prohibited unless staff explicitly permits it. Players should also discuss Dynamaxing with other participants beforehand when its immense size could significantly affect the surrounding environment.

Upon Dynamaxing, a Pokémon is cured of its existing status conditions and remains transformed for THREE ROUNDS before returning to its normal size. Only one Pokémon may Dynamax per thread. These restrictions may be adjusted when everyone involved agrees.

Dynamax grants Pokémon significantly increased strength and durability, access to Max Moves and a powerful Dynamax Barrier. Due to their immense size, Dynamaxed Pokémon cannot ordinarily dodge attacks and must instead rely on their durability and defensive capabilities.

The Dynamax Barrier provides substantial protection against incoming attacks. However, it is not indestructible. Repeated attacks, powerful techniques and strategic approaches can weaken or overcome the barrier. Its effectiveness depends on the Pokémon's Tier, the strength of incoming attacks and the circumstances of the battle.

Dynamax does not eliminate injuries or exhaustion, and Pokémon cannot maintain unlimited protection simply by relying on their barrier.

Gigantamax is a special variation of Dynamax that changes a Pokémon's appearance and grants access to species-exclusive G-Max Moves. Eligible Pokémon must unlock Gigantamax through the appropriate Pokémart purchase. Gigantamax follows the same general rules and restrictions as Dynamax.
