Pokémon Abilities function on Stellara, although their effects are adapted to accommodate the freeform, narrative-focused battle system. Rather than maintaining individually modified descriptions, Stellara uses the canonical Pokémon Abilities and their established effects as a reference.

Players may select any Ability that their Pokémon can legitimately possess, subject to the appropriate rules and restrictions. Custom Abilities are not permitted.

Abilities retain their defining characteristics, strengths, weaknesses and activation conditions wherever reasonably possible. However, their effects are interpreted narratively rather than through exact video game percentages, damage calculations or other mechanics that do not translate appropriately to roleplay.

Abilities that improve strength, speed, durability or other attributes must remain appropriate to the Pokémon's Tier. Their effects cannot be stacked indefinitely with moves or other Abilities to create unreasonable advantages or overcome overwhelming differences in strength.

Defensive Abilities and immunities retain their intended protections, but they do not necessarily make Pokémon invulnerable to every form of harm. Likewise, Abilities that restrict movement, disable attacks or interfere with opponents must allow reasonable opportunities for counterplay.

Healing Abilities may help Pokémon recover from injuries or status conditions according to their intended effects. However, healing injuries does not replenish expended energy, eliminate exhaustion or allow Pokémon to recover indefinitely without appropriate limitations.

Abilities that involve transformations, copying other Pokémon, changing forms or manipulating the battlefield must also respect Pokémon Tiers and the circumstances of the roleplay. They cannot be used to bypass progression restrictions or grant capabilities beyond what the Pokémon can reasonably possess.

Above all, Abilities should enhance a Pokémon's identity and provide meaningful advantages without guaranteeing victory. Players are expected to use them fairly and reasonably, just as they would any move or other combat technique.

For reference, players may consult the canonical Pokémon Ability list. Stellara's rules take precedence whenever a canonical mechanic conflicts with the site's narrative combat system.
