Special Battle Mechanics allow Pokémon to temporarily access extraordinary powers that can significantly influence the outcome of a battle. Stellara supports Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, Dynamax, Gigantamax and Terastallization, each with its own requirements and limitations.

These mechanics do not increase a Pokémon's Tier, nor can they be used to overcome overwhelming differences in strength. Their effects must remain reasonable and consistent with Stellara's Battle Rules. Injuries and exhaustion continue to affect Pokémon regardless of the powers they possess.

Pokémon cannot combine multiple Special Battle Mechanics simultaneously. During ordinary roleplay, Trainers may use different mechanics within the same thread, provided each mechanic's individual restrictions are followed.

During official League battles, Trainers are generally restricted to ONE SPECIAL BATTLE MECHANIC per battle. This restriction may be adjusted if all participants agree beforehand. Staff may also establish additional restrictions for official competitions, tournaments and special events.
