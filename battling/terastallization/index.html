Terastallization is a temporary transformation that changes a Pokémon's typing and gives it a crystallized appearance. Originally discovered in Paldea, Terastallization was introduced to Stellara through collaboration between Paldean and Stellaran scientists, leading to the cultivation of Tera Crystals throughout the region.

To Terastallize a Pokémon, Trainers must purchase a Tera Orb from the Pokémart. Purchasing a Tera Orb also grants one Tera Type for a Pokémon of the Trainer's choice.

Additional Tera Types may be unlocked by purchasing Tera Shards from the Pokémart. These purchases are specific to individual Pokémon and cannot be transferred freely between them.

Terastallization changes a Pokémon's defensive typing to its selected Tera Type and enhances attacks matching that type. Moves matching the Pokémon's Tera Type generally receive a ONE-CATEGORY DAMAGE INCREASE.

If the Tera Type also matches the Pokémon's natural typing, the Pokémon receives an additional narrative advantage when using moves of that type. However, this does not automatically grant another damage-category increase.

Terastallization does not increase a Pokémon's Tier or allow it to ignore reasonable defenses, type effectiveness or other established battle limitations.

Only one Pokémon may Terastallize per thread, although this restriction may be adjusted if everyone involved agrees.
