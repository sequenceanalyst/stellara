Mega Evolution is a temporary transformation that significantly enhances a Pokémon's capabilities and may alter its appearance, typing and Ability.

To Mega Evolve a Pokémon, a Trainer must possess a Key Stone and the appropriate Mega Stone. Key Stones may be incorporated into bracelets, necklaces or other accessories, while Mega Stones must be obtained by completing Missions.

Trainers must possess at least THREE GREAT-TIER POKÉMON to unlock Mega Evolution. The Pokémon being Mega Evolved must also be fully evolved and at least Great Tier.

On Stellara, every fully evolved Pokémon has the potential to Mega Evolve, including species without canonical Mega Evolutions. Custom Mega Forms must be approved by staff before they can be used. Once approved, these forms become available to other eligible players.

Mega Evolution places considerable physical and emotional strain on Pokémon that are unfamiliar with the transformation. Initially, Pokémon may struggle to control their enhanced abilities, but they gradually become more proficient through experience and roleplay. Mastering Mega Evolution does not require a fixed number of transformations.

Mega Evolution substantially enhances a Pokémon's abilities without increasing its actual Tier. Only one Pokémon may Mega Evolve per thread, although this restriction may be adjusted if everyone involved agrees.

Mega Evolution requires a full turn to activate, during which the Pokémon cannot attack. Trainers may possess multiple Mega Stones, but each must be obtained through its own Mission. Mega Stones cannot be traded between players.
