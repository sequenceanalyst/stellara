Talas Co. Power Plant is an abandoned industrial facility formerly operated by Talas Company, one of Stellara's major energy providers. The plant became infamous following the Talas Incident, in which Electric-type Pokémon subjected to prolonged mistreatment and dangerous working conditions revolted against the facility's operators. The resulting destruction forced the power plant to shut down permanently and severely damaged the company's public reputation. Years later, much of the facility remains in disrepair, with damaged machinery, exposed electrical wiring and deteriorating structures scattered throughout the property. Wild Pokémon have since taken up residence inside the abandoned plant, including several aggressive Electric types that have made portions of the facility especially dangerous. Although public access is heavily discouraged, the power plant continues to attract curious Trainers and individuals interested in investigating its history. The Talas Incident remains a controversial chapter in Stellara's industrial history, and its consequences continue to affect the company's reputation.
