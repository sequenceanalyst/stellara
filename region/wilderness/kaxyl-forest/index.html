Kaxyl Forest is the largest forest in Stellara, stretching across miles of densely wooded terrain. The forest is known for its towering trees, thick undergrowth and winding dirt paths that connect several surrounding settlements. Much of Kaxyl Forest receives relatively little sunlight due to its dense canopy, leaving certain areas shrouded in shadows even during the middle of the day. A wide variety of wild Pokémon inhabit the forest, particularly Bug, Grass and other species that thrive in woodland environments. Although the established trails make traveling through Kaxyl Forest relatively straightforward, venturing deeper into the wilderness can be difficult due to the dense vegetation and confusing terrain. The forest is also home to numerous clearings, streams and secluded areas that remain largely undisturbed by human activity, making it a popular destination for researchers and adventurous Trainers.
