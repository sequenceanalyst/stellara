Elysia Falls is a popular natural attraction located near Hamal Town, best known for its beautiful waterfall and numerous geothermal hot springs. The area consists of rocky pools, flowing streams and naturally heated waters surrounded by lush vegetation. Steam frequently rises from the hot springs, creating a thin veil of mist that gives the surrounding landscape a peaceful atmosphere. Many visitors travel to Elysia Falls to relax in its mineral-rich waters, which are widely believed to have therapeutic properties. The hot springs are also popular among wild Pokémon, and it is not uncommon to see various species resting near the pools or enjoying the warmth. Although most of the accessible springs are safe for bathing, certain pools can reach dangerously high temperatures and are best avoided. The combination of its natural beauty and relaxing atmosphere has made Elysia Falls one of Stellara's most popular destinations for people looking to escape the demands of everyday life.
