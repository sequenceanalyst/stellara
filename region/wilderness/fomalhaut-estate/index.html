Fomalhaut Estate is an abandoned manor located near Kaxyl Forest, not too far from Largia Swamp. The estate has a notorious reputation throughout Stellara due to the countless ghost stories and unexplained occurrences associated with the property. Its once-impressive mansion has fallen into disrepair, with overgrown gardens, deteriorating structures and neglected courtyards scattered throughout the grounds. Visitors have reported hearing strange noises coming from inside the mansion, seeing mysterious figures wandering its hallways and experiencing other unusual phenomena. Dark clouds also seem to gather above the estate with remarkable frequency, giving the property an unsettling atmosphere even during the daytime. Despite its reputation, Fomalhaut Estate continues to attract paranormal investigators and curious Trainers interested in uncovering its mysteries. However, very little is known about what actually takes place within the mansion, and most locals strongly discourage visitors from exploring the property alone.
