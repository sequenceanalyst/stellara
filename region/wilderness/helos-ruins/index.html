Helos Ruins are the remains of an ancient civilization located in Northern Stellara, near the Gurios Mountains. The site consists of several complexes containing deteriorating houses, stone towers and other structures whose original purposes remain uncertain. Much of the area has been overtaken by vegetation and wild Pokémon, although portions of the architecture have survived remarkably well despite their age. Numerous inscriptions, carvings and artifacts have been discovered throughout the ruins, providing valuable information about Stellara's distant past. Archaeologists and historians regularly conduct excavations at the site, and many of their discoveries are displayed in museums throughout Nair City. However, large sections of Helos Ruins remain unexplored, with researchers continuing to uncover evidence of the civilization that once inhabited the area. Due to its historical importance, certain sections of the ruins are protected from unauthorized excavation and other activities that could damage the surviving structures.
