Ulan Grotto is an extensive network of underground caves located beneath the Vaisera Mountains. The grotto consists of winding tunnels, spacious caverns and narrow passageways that extend deep beneath the mountain range. Certain sections are illuminated by naturally occurring crystals that glow in different colors, creating beautiful displays of light against the surrounding rock formations. However, much of Ulan Grotto remains completely dark, and its complicated layout makes it easy for unfamiliar travelers to become lost. Various species of cave-dwelling Pokémon inhabit the tunnels, including some that rarely venture aboveground. The grotto has also attracted the attention of geologists and researchers interested in studying its unusual mineral formations. Although several sections have been mapped and are relatively safe to explore, other passages remain poorly documented, and visitors are strongly encouraged to bring proper equipment or travel with experienced guides.
