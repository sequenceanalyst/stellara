Vardos Badlands is a rugged landscape located along the northwestern outskirts of Deneb Desert, where the sandy terrain gradually gives way to rocky plateaus and deep canyons. The area is known for its unusual sandstone formations, steep ravines and exposed layers of colorful sediment that have been shaped by erosion over thousands of years. Vegetation is relatively scarce, although hardy shrubs and other drought-resistant plants can be found in sheltered areas. Vardos Badlands is particularly interesting to geologists and paleontologists, as its exposed rock formations occasionally reveal fossils and other evidence of prehistoric life. Several abandoned mining settlements can also be found throughout the region, remnants of earlier attempts to extract valuable minerals from the surrounding landscape. Although the badlands attract researchers and adventurous Trainers, navigating the narrow canyons and unstable cliffs can be difficult, especially in areas where established trails are nonexistent.
