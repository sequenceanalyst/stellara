Ignara Highlands is a volcanic region located along the southern reaches of the Vaisera Mountains. The area was shaped by ancient volcanic activity and is characterized by dark basalt formations, rocky plateaus and remnants of long-cooled lava flows. At the center of the highlands lies a dormant volcano that continues to produce geothermal activity, with steam vents, naturally heated pools and mineral-rich deposits scattered throughout the surrounding terrain. Although the volcano has not erupted in recent history, certain areas remain hazardous due to unstable ground and extremely high temperatures near geothermal openings. Ignara Highlands is home to a variety of Fire, Rock and Ground-type Pokémon that have adapted to its unusual environment. The region also attracts geologists and researchers interested in studying its volcanic history and geothermal features. Despite its rugged appearance, patches of hardy vegetation have gradually established themselves across the older lava fields, creating a distinctive landscape where volcanic rock and plant life coexist.
