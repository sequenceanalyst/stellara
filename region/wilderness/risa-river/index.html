Risa River is one of Stellara's most important waterways, flowing westward from Polaris Lake before eventually emptying into the ocean near Eridani City. The river winds through several different landscapes, providing freshwater to the surrounding ecosystems and settlements along its banks. Certain stretches are calm enough for boating and fishing, while others contain powerful currents and rocky rapids that can be difficult to navigate. Risa River is also known for the colorful stones found along portions of its shoreline, which have become popular among collectors and visitors. Numerous species of Water-type Pokémon inhabit the river, and some of its calmer sections are frequently used for Pokémon racing competitions. Several bridges and transportation routes cross the waterway, connecting settlements on opposite sides of the river. Its importance to transportation, recreation and the surrounding environment has made Risa River one of Stellara's most recognizable geographical features.
