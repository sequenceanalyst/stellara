Irra Beach is a beautiful stretch of coastline located along Eastern Stellara, near the resort city of Vega. The beach is famous for its soft sand, colorful seashells and remarkably clear blue waters, making it one of the region's most popular vacation destinations. Several resorts, rental cottages and recreational facilities can be found along the shoreline, attracting tourists from across Stellara and beyond. Swimming, surfing, snorkeling and other water-related activities are especially popular, and the beach hosts several festivals and sporting competitions throughout the year. Wild Water-type Pokémon are a common sight near the shore, with many species frequently swimming alongside visitors or resting in the shallower waters. Although Irra Beach is generally considered safe, certain areas are subject to strong currents and occasional encounters with more aggressive Pokémon. Nevertheless, its warm atmosphere and scenic coastline have made it one of Stellara's most recognizable recreational destinations.
