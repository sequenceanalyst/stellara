The Breshno Flatlands are a sprawling stretch of grasslands characterized by rolling hills, colorful wildflowers and wide-open meadows. The area is home to a diverse population of wild Pokémon, with Grass, Normal, Electric and Flying types being especially common throughout the landscape. Much of the terrain consists of tall grass and gently sloping hills, although scattered groves of trees, ponds and small streams can also be found. During the warmer months, flowers of nearly every color bloom across the flatlands, attracting visitors who come to enjoy the scenery. The Breshno Flatlands are also a popular destination for newer Trainers due to their relatively accessible terrain and abundance of wild Pokémon. However, certain areas are inhabited by more powerful Pokémon, particularly farther away from the established trails.
