Polaris Lake is the largest lake in Stellara, covering an impressive area and supporting a diverse population of aquatic Pokémon. Several settlements are located near its shores, and the lake serves as an important source of freshwater for much of the surrounding region. Fishing, boating and other recreational activities are common, particularly around the more developed portions of the shoreline. Polaris Lake is also the source of the Risa River, which flows westward across Stellara before eventually reaching the ocean. Despite its importance to the region, the lake has developed a mysterious reputation due to longstanding stories involving a gigantic Pokémon that supposedly inhabits its deepest waters. According to local legends, the creature is powerful enough to overturn large vessels with a single movement of its tail. Although no definitive evidence of its existence has been discovered, many fishermen remain cautious about venturing too far from shore.
