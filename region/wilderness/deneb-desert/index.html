Within this location: Salaris Flats

Deneb Desert is a harsh, arid region located in Southern Stellara, known for its scorching temperatures, rolling sand dunes and ancient ruins scattered throughout the landscape. Much of the desert consists of barren terrain with very little vegetation, although occasional oases and rocky formations provide shelter for the Pokémon that inhabit the area. One of its more unusual landmarks is Salaris Flats, a broad salt basin formed by the evaporation of an ancient lake. During rare periods of heavy rainfall, a thin layer of water can accumulate across the basin, creating a mirror-like reflection of the sky. Deneb Desert is also home to several archaeological sites, many of which contain remnants of civilizations that existed long before modern Stellara. Researchers and treasure hunters regularly venture into the desert in search of historical discoveries, often using Saiph City as their base of operations. However, the extreme climate and limited water supplies make prolonged exploration dangerous without proper preparation.
