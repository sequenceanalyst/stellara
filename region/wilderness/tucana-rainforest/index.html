Tucana Rainforest is a tropical wilderness characterized by its humid climate, dense vegetation and remarkable variety of plant and Pokémon species. Towering trees form a thick canopy above the forest floor, while vines, ferns and other tropical plants flourish throughout the undergrowth. Frequent rainfall keeps the environment warm and damp, allowing a wide range of species to thrive. However, not all of the rainforest's vegetation is harmless, as several plants possess toxic properties or other adaptations that can pose a danger to unfamiliar travelers. Scattered throughout Tucana Rainforest are several small villages, some of which preserve structures and traditions associated with communities that inhabited the area generations ago. The rainforest has become an important destination for biological research, particularly among scientists studying rare plants and Pokémon. Although certain trails provide access to its more populated areas, much of Tucana Rainforest remains difficult to navigate due to the thick vegetation and unpredictable wildlife.
