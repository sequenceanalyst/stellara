Largia Swamp is a vast wetland located near Kaxyl Forest and Fomalhaut Estate. The swamp is known for its muddy terrain, murky pools of water and twisted trees whose exposed roots stretch across much of the landscape. A layer of greenish fog frequently settles over the wetlands, making it difficult to see very far ahead and giving the area an especially unwelcoming appearance. Despite its unpleasant reputation, Largia Swamp supports a surprisingly diverse ecosystem, with Poison, Water and Grass-type Pokémon being particularly common. Many of its plants have developed unusual adaptations to survive in the damp environment, including several species valued for their medicinal or toxic properties. Researchers from nearby Sabik Town frequently visit the swamp to study its wildlife and vegetation. However, traveling through Largia Swamp can be dangerous, as unstable ground, concealed pools and territorial Pokémon make navigation difficult for inexperienced visitors.
