Within this location: Nymira Mangroves

Yanna Jungle covers much of Antila Island and is known for its tropical climate, colorful vegetation and extraordinary biodiversity. The jungle is home to a wide variety of wild Pokémon, with rivers and streams winding between its densely wooded areas. Near the center of Yanna Jungle is an impressive waterfall surrounded by natural pools, making it a popular destination for visitors interested in swimming and other recreational activities. Along the island's sheltered coastline, the jungle transitions into Nymira Mangroves, a network of partially submerged trees, tidal channels and shallow lagoons where freshwater mixes with the ocean. These coastal wetlands support numerous aquatic Pokémon and provide an important habitat for species that thrive in brackish water. Although many parts of Yanna Jungle are relatively accessible, certain areas are controlled by powerful territorial Pokémon that aggressively defend their habitats. As a result, travelers are encouraged to remain cautious when exploring the jungle's more remote regions.
