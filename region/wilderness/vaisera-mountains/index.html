Within this location: Veyra Moorlands

The Vaisera Mountains are one of Stellara's two major mountain ranges, occupying a considerable portion of the region's western territory. Unlike the frigid Gurios Mountains to the north, the Vaisera Mountains experience a generally warmer climate, although temperatures become noticeably cooler at higher elevations. The lower foothills are home to the Veyra Moorlands, an open landscape of grassy hills, wildflowers and scattered rocky formations. Farther into the mountains, the terrain becomes increasingly rugged, with steep cliffs, narrow trails and secluded valleys separating the peaks. The Vaisera Mountains are particularly popular among hikers, martial artists and Trainers seeking challenging environments in which to improve their abilities. Several trails begin near Pavo Town, although the more remote areas are inhabited by powerful Pokémon and can be difficult to navigate. The mountain range is also home to several cave systems, including the extensive underground passages of Ulan Grotto.
