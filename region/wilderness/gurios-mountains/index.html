Within this location: Borealis Tundra

The Gurios Mountains are a frigid mountain range located in Northern Stellara, famous for their snow-covered peaks, massive glaciers and harsh weather conditions. Much of the region experiences freezing temperatures throughout the year, although the climate and vegetation vary considerably depending on elevation. Above the tree line lies Borealis Tundra, a windswept alpine environment where hardy grasses, mosses and small flowering plants survive during the brief warmer seasons. Farther up the mountains, the landscape gives way to steep cliffs, deep snowfields and glaciers that remain frozen year-round. The Gurios Mountains are a popular destination for winter sports enthusiasts and experienced Trainers seeking challenging environments in which to train their Pokémon. However, avalanches, sudden snowstorms and unstable ice formations make certain areas extremely dangerous. Several established trails and recreational facilities can be found near Nair City, while the higher elevations remain largely inaccessible to ordinary travelers.
