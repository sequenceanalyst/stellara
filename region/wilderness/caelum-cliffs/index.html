Caelum Cliffs is a dramatic stretch of coastline located in Western Stellara, not too far from Eridani City. The area is characterized by towering sea cliffs, rocky beaches and isolated coves that have been shaped by centuries of erosion. Powerful waves frequently crash against the shoreline, carving caves and narrow passages into the surrounding rock formations. Some of these sea caves are accessible by boat, while others can only be explored during low tide when the water temporarily recedes. Several offshore rock formations also provide nesting grounds for Flying-type Pokémon, while numerous Water and Rock types inhabit the coastal waters. Caelum Cliffs is a popular destination for experienced climbers, marine researchers and Trainers interested in exploring its natural formations. However, the steep terrain and unpredictable ocean currents make certain portions of the coastline difficult to access, and visitors are advised to exercise caution when exploring the caves.
