The Stellara Pokémon League is a massive complex that serves as the administrative center for official Pokémon battling and Trainer-related organizations throughout the region. Designed to function as a self-contained city, the League contains numerous stadiums, training facilities, residential accommodations, restaurants and other amenities intended to support Trainers and visitors from across the world. The League is particularly famous for hosting the Masters' Tournament, a prestigious competition in which qualified Trainers compete alongside Stellara's Gym Leaders for an opportunity to challenge the reigning Champion. Trainers must first earn eight Gym Badges to qualify, although simply meeting this requirement does not guarantee success against the region's strongest competitors. The League also oversees official Gym operations and maintains the standards governing competitive Pokémon battles throughout Stellara. With its enormous stadiums and advanced facilities, the Stellara Pokémon League is widely regarded as one of the region's most impressive landmarks.
