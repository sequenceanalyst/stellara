Altair City is a large city located on an elevated plateau in Western Stellara, not too far from the Vaisera Mountains. The city is known for its long history of competitive Pokémon battling, with numerous training facilities, stadiums and battle academies scattered throughout its districts. Many accomplished Trainers visit Altair City to improve their skills, and some of Stellara's most successful competitive Trainers have called the city home. Despite its reputation for being a competitive city, Altair City has plenty to offer ordinary visitors, including shopping districts, restaurants and scenic overlooks that provide excellent views of the surrounding landscape.
