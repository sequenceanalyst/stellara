Antares City is a major city built within a dense tropical jungle, making it one of the most architecturally distinctive settlements in Stellara. Despite its large population and numerous towering buildings, the city has been carefully designed to coexist with the surrounding environment rather than replace it. Many of its structures incorporate natural vegetation, elevated walkways and open spaces that allow the jungle's ecosystem to remain largely intact. Wild Pokémon are a common sight throughout Antares City, and residents have grown accustomed to sharing their surroundings with the many species that inhabit the area. The city is also an important center for environmental conservation and tropical research, with numerous organizations dedicated to protecting the jungle's biodiversity. Although the construction of such a large city initially raised concerns about its environmental impact, Antares City has become a prominent example of how modern urban development can coexist with natural ecosystems.
