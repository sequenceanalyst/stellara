Cyngi City is the largest city in Stellara and serves as the region's primary commercial, financial and cultural center. Located south of the Risa River, the city is a sprawling metropolis home to over a million people and Pokémon, with numerous skyscrapers, shopping districts and residential neighborhoods spread throughout its boundaries. Many of Stellara's largest corporations have established their headquarters in Cyngi City, which also serves as the administrative center for the regional government. Tourism is another major industry, with visitors coming from around the world to experience the city's entertainment venues, restaurants, museums and cultural attractions. Despite its enormous population and busy atmosphere, Cyngi City maintains numerous parks and recreational areas where residents can escape the bustle of everyday life. As the region's most prominent city, Cyngi City is often one of the first destinations for people visiting Stellara.
