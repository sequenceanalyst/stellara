Nair City is the largest city in Northern Stellara and is located near the Gurios Mountains. Due to its northern location and proximity to the mountain range, the city experiences relatively cold temperatures throughout the year, with heavy snowfall being particularly common during the winter months. Nair City is a popular destination for winter sports, including skiing, snowboarding and mountain climbing, and many visitors travel here specifically to explore the surrounding wilderness. The city is also known for its numerous museums and historical institutions, many of which contain artifacts recovered from the nearby Helos Ruins. These collections have helped establish Nair City as an important center for historical research and education. Despite its cold climate, the city has a lively outdoor culture, with residents frequently participating in recreational activities throughout the year. Its combination of historical attractions and mountainous scenery makes Nair City a popular destination for tourists and researchers alike.
