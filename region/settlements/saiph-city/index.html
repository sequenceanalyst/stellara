Saiph City is one of the largest cities in Southern Stellara and is located deep within the Deneb Desert. Commonly referred to as the "Oasis of Deneb Desert," the city is one of the few major settlements capable of supporting a large population in the region's harsh desert environment. Saiph City's infrastructure has been specifically designed to withstand extreme temperatures, with advanced cooling systems and carefully managed water supplies making everyday life considerably more comfortable. The city is particularly well known for its archaeological research, as the surrounding desert contains numerous ancient ruins and other historical discoveries. Saiph City is home to a major research laboratory specializing in ancient Pokémon, fossils and artifacts recovered from the desert. As a result, the city attracts archaeologists, paleontologists and treasure hunters from across the world. Despite its remote location, Saiph City maintains a thriving economy with numerous markets, restaurants and cultural attractions that reflect its distinctive desert heritage.
