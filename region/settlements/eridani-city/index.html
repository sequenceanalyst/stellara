Eridani City is a major seaport located along the western coast of Stellara, near where the Risa River empties into the ocean. Once a struggling industrial city, Eridani experienced a prolonged economic decline due to outsourcing and the closure of several major businesses. However, extensive investments and redevelopment efforts have transformed the city into one of the fastest-growing metropolitan areas in Stellara. Eridani City is particularly well known for its maritime industries, seafood restaurants, cruise services and vibrant music scene, with numerous entertainment venues scattered throughout its waterfront districts. The city is also home to the headquarters of Talas Company, one of Stellara's major energy providers. Despite its economic importance, Talas Company continues to face public scrutiny following revelations of Pokémon mistreatment at its former power plant. Although Eridani City has made considerable progress in revitalizing its economy, the controversy remains an important part of its recent history.
