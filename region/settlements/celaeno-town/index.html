Celaeno Town is a picturesque town situated within a fertile valley in Central Stellara. The town is known for its beautiful gardens, colorful flowers and peaceful atmosphere, making it a popular destination for people looking to escape the hustle and bustle of the region's larger cities. Many of Celaeno Town's residents are involved in gardening, horticulture and traditional craftsmanship, and the town hosts several festivals throughout the year to celebrate its local culture. Fairy-type Pokémon are especially common in and around Celaeno Town, and the residents have a longstanding tradition of living alongside them.
