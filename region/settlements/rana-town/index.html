Rana Town is a rural settlement located near Polaris Lake and is widely regarded as the agricultural center of Stellara. The town is surrounded by extensive farmland, ranches and open pastures, with many residents making their living through agriculture and Pokémon breeding. Rana Town produces a significant portion of the region's agricultural goods, and its farmers frequently work alongside Pokémon to cultivate crops and maintain their properties. The town is also a popular destination for aspiring Pokémon Breeders, with several breeding facilities and training programs available to those interested in the profession. Although Rana Town has retained much of its rural charm, improvements to its infrastructure have made it increasingly accessible to visitors and businesses. Pokémon racing is another longstanding local tradition, with regular competitions attracting participants from across Stellara. Some Pokémon are so well known among residents that they are practically considered honorary members of the community.
