Musca Town is a medium-sized town located in Southwestern Stellara. The town is particularly well known for its craftsmanship and longstanding martial arts traditions, both of which have been influenced by the many different cultures that have settled here over the years. As a result, Musca Town has a unique architectural style, with colorful buildings, stone-paved streets and numerous open-air courtyards scattered throughout the town. Many of its residents work as artisans, producing everything from pottery and furniture to specialized equipment for Trainers and their Pokémon. Musca Town is also home to several renowned martial arts schools, attracting people from across Stellara who want to learn different fighting disciplines.
