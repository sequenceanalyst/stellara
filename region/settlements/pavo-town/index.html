Pavo Town is a medium-sized town located at the base of the Vaisera Mountains in Western Stellara. The town serves as a popular stopping point for travelers exploring the mountain range, with numerous inns, restaurants and specialty shops catering to hikers and Trainers. Most of Pavo Town's buildings are relatively small, rarely exceeding three stories in height, giving the settlement a more traditional appearance despite its modern infrastructure. Hiking, rock climbing and other outdoor activities are particularly popular among residents, and several trails leading into the Vaisera Mountains begin near the town's outskirts. Pavo Town is also home to a number of experienced mountain guides who help visitors navigate the region's more dangerous terrain. The town's Gym is another major attraction, drawing challengers from across Stellara who want to test their abilities before continuing their journeys.
