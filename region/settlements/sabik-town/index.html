Sabik Town is a medium-sized settlement located along the outskirts of Largia Swamp. Due to its proximity to the wetlands, the town is surrounded by marshes, ponds and dense vegetation, with elevated walkways connecting some of its neighborhoods. Sabik Town is particularly well known for its research into Poison-type Pokémon, medicinal plants and the unique ecosystems found throughout Largia Swamp. Several research facilities operate within the town, attracting scientists and Pokémon researchers from across the region. Despite the swamp's reputation for being dangerous, Sabik Town is a relatively safe place to live, largely thanks to the residents' extensive knowledge of the local environment.
