Shaula Town is a small, nature-oriented settlement located near Kaxyl Forest. The town is known for its traditional architecture, with many homes and buildings constructed from locally sourced wood and other natural materials. Unlike most settlements in Stellara, Shaula Town places relatively little emphasis on modern technology, with residents preferring a simpler lifestyle that encourages closer connections with nature. However, the town still maintains essential modern conveniences, including electricity, plumbing and communication services. Numerous gardens, ponds, parks and nature trails are scattered throughout Shaula Town, and wild Pokémon are frequently seen wandering near its residential areas. The town is particularly popular among Trainers interested in wilderness survival, with experienced guides offering lessons on navigation, camping and living alongside wild Pokémon. Although Shaula Town may seem isolated compared to Stellara's larger settlements, its residents take great pride in their close relationship with the surrounding environment.
