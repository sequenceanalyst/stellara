Vega City is Stellara's largest resort city, located along the eastern coastline near Irra Beach. The city is famous for its luxurious hotels, casinos, entertainment venues and vibrant nightlife, attracting enormous numbers of tourists throughout the year. Many of Vega City's streets are lined with brightly illuminated buildings, restaurants and shopping centers, while its coastal districts provide convenient access to the beaches and recreational activities nearby. The city also hosts numerous concerts, festivals and other entertainment events, making it one of the most popular vacation destinations in the region. However, beneath Vega City's glamorous reputation lies a less welcoming side, with organized crime, illegal gambling and other questionable activities operating behind the scenes. Although most visitors experience little more than the city's excitement and hospitality, those who venture into its less reputable districts may discover that Vega City has more to offer than its advertisements suggest.
