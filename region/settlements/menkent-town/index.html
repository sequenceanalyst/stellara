Menkent Town is a small, historic town located near the outskirts of Kaxyl Forest, not too far from Fomalhaut Estate. The town is known for its old-fashioned architecture, cobblestone streets and numerous stories involving Ghost-type Pokémon. Many of its buildings have stood for generations, and the residents take great pride in preserving the town's history. Menkent Town has a reputation for being haunted, although the locals generally don't seem bothered by the unusual occurrences that take place there. In fact, paranormal tourism is one of the town's largest industries, with visitors coming from all over Stellara to investigate its mysteries.
