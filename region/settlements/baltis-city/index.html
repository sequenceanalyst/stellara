Baltis City is a large city located along the shores of Polaris Lake and is particularly well known for its frequent rainfall and powerful thunderstorms. Due to the region's unusually wet climate, the city has developed an extensive drainage system designed to prevent flooding during periods of heavy precipitation. Numerous artificial ponds, canals and streams run throughout Baltis City, serving both practical and recreational purposes while contributing to its distinctive appearance. Many of the city's parks and public spaces have been built around these waterways, and residents frequently spend time along their shores when the weather permits. Polaris Lake is another major attraction, providing opportunities for fishing, boating and other water-related activities. Although visitors may initially find the city's weather inconvenient, Baltis City's residents have adapted remarkably well to the frequent storms, and the city's unique relationship with water has become an important part of its identity.
