Hamal Town is a small settlement located near Elysia Falls, a popular natural attraction known for its hot springs and geothermal activity. The town is surrounded by numerous hot springs, geysers and pools of naturally heated water, many of which have been incorporated into public bathhouses and private resorts. A thin layer of mist frequently hangs over Hamal Town due to the constant release of steam, giving the settlement a distinctive atmosphere. Tourism is one of the town's largest industries, with visitors traveling from across Stellara to relax in its hot springs and enjoy the surrounding scenery. The mineral-rich waters are widely believed to have therapeutic properties, although some of the more extraordinary claims about their healing abilities remain unproven. Despite its popularity, Hamal Town has retained a relatively peaceful atmosphere, making it an ideal destination for people looking to relax away from the region's larger cities.
