Mintaka City is a major city located in Northeastern Stellara and is widely regarded as one of the region's leading centers for scientific research and technological innovation. The city is home to numerous universities, research laboratories and technology companies, many of which specialize in robotics, artificial intelligence and the study of Pokémon behavior. Mintaka City is also known for its modern infrastructure, with an extensive public transportation system and many advanced technologies incorporated into everyday life. Despite its emphasis on technological development, the city has plenty of recreational attractions, including museums, shopping districts and public parks. Many aspiring scientists and researchers move to Mintaka City to pursue their careers.
