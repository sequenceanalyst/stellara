Ultra Beasts and Paradox Pokémon

Ultra Beasts and Paradox Pokémon originate from other dimensions, distant timelines and unusual environments. Some have found their way into Stellara through dimensional tears and temporal anomalies, allowing Trainers to encounter them in designated locations.

Ultra Beasts and non-Legendary Paradox Pokémon may be encountered and captured through Stellara's ordinary catching system when their respective locations become available. Their Tiers are determined through the weighted wild encounter system, and capturing Elite-tier or higher Pokémon still requires staff approval.

Legendary Paradox Pokémon are subject to their established Capture Categories and cannot be obtained through ordinary wild encounters unless explicitly permitted by staff.

Poképasses cannot bypass these restrictions or guarantee encounters with Pokémon that are unavailable in a particular location.

Legendary and Mythical Pokémon

Legendary and Mythical Pokémon are among the rarest Pokémon in existence, with many possessing extraordinary abilities and powers. They can be encountered throughout Stellara, often playing important roles in major events, Missions and storylines. Staff will generally be responsible for portraying these Pokémon during significant encounters.

Not every Legendary Pokémon can be captured. Some may be battled, befriended or encountered through storylines without ever becoming available for ownership. Each Legendary Pokémon has its own personality and motivations, and Trainers should not expect every encounter to end in a battle or capture.

Wild Legendary Pokémon are generally considered Legendary Tier unless otherwise specified by staff. However, Legendary Pokémon owned by players follow Stellara's normal Tier progression system. Their starting Tier is determined by staff based on their species, capabilities, circumstances of capture and the Trainer's progression. Legendary status alone does not grant superiority over Pokémon of comparable Tiers.

Capture Categories

Legendary and Mythical Pokémon are divided into four Capture Categories, which determine how they may be obtained. These categories do not represent their combat strength.

Forbidden: Pokémon that cannot be permanently captured or owned by players.

Restricted: Pokémon that are normally unavailable for capture. Permanent ownership is only possible through extraordinary circumstances and explicit staff approval.

Exceptional: Pokémon that may be captured through major staff-approved storylines and exceptional accomplishments.

Permitted: Pokémon that may be obtained through approved storylines, events or special achievements. Their requirements are generally less demanding than those of Exceptional Pokémon.

All Legendary and Mythical Pokémon retain their Capture Category after being obtained. Trading or transferring ownership of these Pokémon requires staff approval. Captured Legendary Pokémon remain subject to ordinary progression requirements, including weekly Tier advancement limits and Trainer Tier restrictions.

The existing Legendary and Mythical Pokémon lists determine which Capture Category each species belongs to.
