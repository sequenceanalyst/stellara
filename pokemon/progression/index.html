On Stellara, Pokémon progression is handled through a tier-based system rather than numerical levels. Instead of requiring Pokémon to participate in battles constantly to become stronger, players can earn EXP simply by roleplaying. This allows Trainers to develop their Pokémon through battles, exploration, social interactions, catching threads and other activities.

EXP belongs to the Trainer rather than individual Pokémon. All EXP earned through roleplay is added to a shared balance, which can be spent on advancing any of the Trainer's Pokémon or purchasing items and upgrades from the Pokémart. Players are free to distribute their EXP however they wish, regardless of which Pokémon participated in the roleplay.

Earning EXP

Players earn 5 EXP for every qualifying in-character post. Completing a thread with at least 10 posts per participant awards an additional 50 EXP to each participant.

These rewards apply to social threads, battle threads, catching threads, solo threads and other ordinary roleplay activities. Players are encouraged to participate in meaningful roleplay rather than creating unnecessary posts or dividing continuous scenes into multiple threads solely to earn additional EXP.

Missions and special events may award additional EXP depending on their difficulty, objectives and rewards. These bonuses are explained in their respective rulesets.

Pokémon Tier Advancement

EXP can be spent to advance Pokémon through Stellara's tier hierarchy. Each tier represents a Pokémon's overall strength, capabilities and experience. The amount of EXP required to advance depends on the tier the Pokémon is advancing into.

Pokémon may only advance ONE TIER PER WEEK, regardless of how much EXP their Trainer has available. Players must purchase each advancement individually and cannot skip tiers.

A Pokémon's maximum tier is also determined by its Trainer's tier. Pokémon may generally be up to one tier higher than their Trainer, although additional restrictions may apply to higher-tier Pokémon. Advancing a Pokémon's tier does not automatically advance its Trainer's tier.

To advance a Pokémon, players must purchase the appropriate tier advancement through the Pokémart and update their Pokémon PC accordingly.

EXP Expenditure

In addition to tier advancement, EXP can be used to purchase moves, evolution items, medicine, battle upgrades and other items available in the Pokémart.

Players are not required to spend EXP equally among their Pokémon. Some Trainers may prefer to invest heavily in a smaller team, while others may choose to develop a larger collection of Pokémon. There are no additional EXP costs or penalties associated with owning more Pokémon.

EXP may also be transferred between players as part of legitimate transactions involving Pokémon or items, subject to staff approval. Unrestricted EXP gifting or transfers intended to circumvent progression requirements are not permitted.
