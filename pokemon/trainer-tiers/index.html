Trainer Tiers represent a Trainer's overall experience, accomplishments, knowledge and abilities as a Pokémon Trainer. There are nine Trainer Tiers in Stellara, ranging from Beginner to Legendary. Unlike Pokémon Tiers, which measure a Pokémon's strength and capabilities, Trainer Tiers reflect the Trainer's personal development throughout their journey.

A Trainer's Tier also determines the strength of Pokémon they can reasonably encounter, catch and train. Pokémon may generally be up to one Tier higher than their Trainer, although additional restrictions may apply to higher-tier Pokémon.

Trainer Tier Progression

Trainer progression is determined entirely through roleplay, character development and narrative accomplishments. There are no experience points, minimum battle counts or specific number of threads required to advance. Instead, Trainers must demonstrate the experience, abilities and accomplishments appropriate for their desired Tier.

Each Trainer Tier represents a different stage of development. The examples below illustrate potential accomplishments and are not mandatory requirements.

  • Beginner: New Trainers who are just beginning their journeys and learning the fundamentals of Pokémon training.
  • Rookie: Trainers who have developed basic competence in training, battling and caring for their Pokémon. Examples include completing a first meaningful challenge, developing trust with a difficult Pokémon or learning from an important defeat.
  • Experienced: Capable Trainers who can independently handle challenging situations and demonstrate effective strategies and teamwork. Examples include independently completing a challenging Mission, overcoming repeated setbacks or significantly improving their training methods.
  • Great: Accomplished Trainers who have demonstrated considerable skill and personal growth. Examples include defeating a formidable opponent through strategy, overcoming a major personal weakness or successfully navigating a dangerous situation.
  • Ultra: Highly skilled Trainers who have demonstrated advanced strategies, exceptional judgment and significant accomplishments. Examples include leading others through a dangerous Mission, accepting substantial responsibility or earning recognition through a major achievement.
  • Elite: Outstanding Trainers whose abilities are comparable to some of Stellara's strongest Trainers. Examples include successfully directing a major operation, overcoming an extraordinary challenge or demonstrating exceptional leadership and training expertise.
  • Champion: Exceptional Trainers who have demonstrated regional mastery and overcome remarkable challenges. Examples include defeating exceptionally powerful opponents, accomplishing something of major regional significance or demonstrating abilities comparable to Stellara's most distinguished Trainers.
  • Master: World-class Trainers whose extraordinary accomplishments, expertise and influence place them among the greatest Trainers in the world. Examples include overcoming challenges that would overwhelm most Champion-tier Trainers or achieving accomplishments of worldwide significance.
  • Legendary: Truly extraordinary Trainers whose achievements have transcended conventional expectations. Examples include overcoming a seemingly insurmountable challenge, accomplishing something that changes the course of history or establishing a legacy remembered for generations.

Ranking Up

When a player believes their Trainer has demonstrated sufficient growth, they may submit a Trainer Tier Promotion Request for staff review. Requests should include relevant roleplay threads and a brief explanation of the Trainer's development and accomplishments.

Staff will evaluate requests based on the character's overall development rather than any single accomplishment. Earlier Tiers generally require less significant achievements, while Ultra Tier and above demand increasingly substantial development and accomplishments. Winning battles, completing Missions or possessing powerful Pokémon does not automatically guarantee promotion.

Trainers may advance regardless of their moral alignment, and their accomplishments do not need to be publicly recognized. Trainer Tiers are also separate from official titles and positions. For example, reaching Champion Tier does not automatically make someone the Pokémon League Champion, nor does earning that title automatically grant Champion Tier.

Trainers may only advance one Tier at a time unless staff approves otherwise. If a promotion request is declined, staff may provide feedback to help the player understand what additional development is needed.

Trainer Tiers and Pokémon Tiers progress independently. Increasing a Pokémon's Tier does not automatically advance its Trainer, and increasing a Trainer's Tier does not automatically strengthen their Pokémon.
