Evolution is the process through which Pokémon undergo a transformation, often changing their appearance, abilities and overall capabilities. Pokémon may evolve once they meet the appropriate requirements for their species. Evolution does not increase a Pokémon's Tier, nor does it automatically heal injuries or restore expended energy.

Tier Requirements

For Pokémon that ordinarily evolve by reaching a certain level, Stellara uses the following Tier requirements based on their canonical evolution levels:

Canonical Evolution LevelRequired Tier
6–15Rookie
16–29Experienced
30–44Great
45+Ultra

These levels are only used to determine evolution requirements. Pokémon on Stellara do not have numerical levels. Pokémon with multiple evolutionary stages must meet the requirements for each evolution individually. Some Pokémon may undergo multiple evolutions within the same Tier.

For example, Caterpie can evolve into Metapod and subsequently Butterfree at Rookie Tier. However, Deino must reach Ultra Tier before evolving into Zweilous and eventually Hydreigon.

Pokémon may evolve during or outside of battle. Evolution briefly interrupts the battle while the transformation occurs, but it does not grant prolonged invulnerability or allow Pokémon to avoid the consequences of injuries and exhaustion.

Special Evolution Methods

Certain Pokémon evolve through special conditions rather than reaching a particular level. These methods are explained below.

Evolution Items: Pokémon that require evolutionary stones or other items may evolve by purchasing the appropriate item from the Pokémart for 50 EXP. The Pokémon must also meet any applicable Tier requirements.

Friendship Evolutions: Pokémon that ordinarily evolve through friendship may instead purchase a Friendship Evolution through the Pokémart for 50 EXP. These Pokémon must be at least Experienced Tier unless otherwise specified. No additional friendship tracking or completed threads are required.

Trade Evolutions: Pokémon that ordinarily evolve through trading may evolve by trading with another player or purchasing a Trade Evolution through the Pokémart for 50 EXP. Pokémon requiring an additional held item must possess that item before evolving. Trading with another player does not require purchasing the Trade Evolution service.

Time-Based Evolutions: Pokémon that evolve during particular times of day must still meet those conditions. For example, Eevee evolves into Espeon during the day and Umbreon at night.

Location-Based Evolutions: Pokémon that evolve in particular environments must visit an appropriate location before evolving. On Stellara, the Vaisera Mountains possess a special magnetic field, while the Tucana Rainforest and Gurios Mountains provide environments suitable for certain evolutionary methods. Equivalent locations or alternative canonical evolution methods may also be used where appropriate.

Branching Evolutions: Pokémon with multiple possible evolutionary forms may evolve into any eligible form, provided the appropriate conditions are met. Pokémon such as Tyrogue and Wurmple do not require numerical stats to determine their evolution.

Nincada and Shedinja: When Nincada evolves into Ninjask, a Shedinja may also appear in the Trainer's PC. Shedinja begins at Beginner Tier and progresses normally.

Regional Evolutions: Pokémon may evolve into eligible regional forms where appropriate. Players must indicate which form their Pokémon is evolving into. Regional variants may also be encountered naturally in the wild.

Other Pokémon with unusual evolutionary methods should follow their established requirements where possible, with reasonable adjustments for Stellara's Tier-based system. Players may consult staff if a particular evolution method is unclear.

Evolution During Battle

Pokémon are allowed to evolve during battle, provided they meet their evolution requirements. However, evolution does not automatically protect a Pokémon from incoming attacks or prevent opponents from taking advantage of the situation.

Evolution also does not heal existing injuries, replenish expended energy or eliminate exhaustion. A Pokémon that was already injured or exhausted before evolving will remain injured or exhausted afterward.

Players are encouraged to use evolution as an opportunity for meaningful character development and storytelling rather than as a way to bypass the normal limitations of combat.
