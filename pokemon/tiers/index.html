Tiers are the only stat used on Pokemon Stellara. Tiers tell how strong and experienced a Pokemon is. Listed below are the available tiers on Stellara.

Beginner

The starting point for every Pokémon and Trainer. Beginners are still learning the fundamentals of battling, training, and working together as a team. Pokémon at this level are young, inexperienced, or only recently accustomed to battling, while their Trainers are still developing their knowledge and command. Mistakes are common, and victories often come through instinct, enthusiasm, or luck rather than refined skill.

Rookie

A step beyond the basics, Rookies have begun to establish themselves as somewhat capable battlers. Their Pokémon have received some amounts of proper training and gained some practical experience, while their Trainers have learned to issue commands, develop minor strategies, and understand their team’s strengths and weaknesses. They may be able to handle inexperienced opponents with relative confidence, but still lack the consistency and experience of seasoned Trainers.

Experienced

Experienced Trainers and Pokémon have spent a considerable amount of time battling and training together. They understand the fundamentals of strategy, type matchups, positioning, and teamwork, and can sometimes adapt when a battle doesn’t go according to plan. Pokémon at this level have implemented reliable techniques into their arsenal, developing greater physical and mental endurance. Experienced Trainers and Pokémon are capable of holding their own against ordinary challengers and may begin making names for themselves in their local communities.

Great

Great Trainers have distinguished themselves from the average battlers through exceptional skill, dedication, and experience. Their Pokémon are well-trained, battle-hardened, and capable of executing sophisticated strategies under pressure. A Great Trainer can turn disadvantages into opportunities and make effective use of their entire team rather than relying solely on individual strength. Trainers at this level may be successful Gym Challengers, tournament competitors, or Gym Trainers.

Ultra

Ultra-level Trainers and Pokémon represent an extraordinary caliber of battling ability. Their teams possess remarkable training, discipline, and coordination. Ultra Trainers are highly adaptable and capable of overwhelming powerful opponents through strategy as well as strength. These are the sorts of Trainers who have become seasoned Gym Leaders, dominate tournaments, or earn recognition throughout several cities in their home region.

Elite

Elite Trainers stand among the most formidable battlers in their region. Their Pokémon are exceptionally mature, experienced, and finely trained, possessing a deep understanding of battle mechanics and an ability to perform under extreme pressure. Elite Trainers rarely rely on a single strategy and can adapt their approach to virtually any opponent. Veteran Gym Leaders, Elite Four members, tournament champions, and renowned specialists fall within this tier.

Champion

Champions have reached a level of mastery that very few Trainers ever achieve. Their Pokémon are exceptionally powerful and battle-tested, while the bond between Trainer and Pokémon allows for near-seamless coordination. Champions possess tremendous tactical awareness, adaptability, and composure, often seeming to anticipate an opponent's actions before they occur. A Champion is capable of defeating even the strongest conventional Trainers and may be considered among the very best battlers in an entire region.

Master

Master-level Trainers represent the absolute pinnacle of Pokémon battling and training. Their Pokémon retain phenomenal strength, experience, discipline, and battle intelligence, while their Trainers have demonstrated the ability to overcome almost any challenge placed before them. Masters are not merely powerful, they are impressively versatile and resilient, commanding teams capable of handling a wide variety of opponents and circumstances. Regional Champions, legendary tournament winners, and the most accomplished Trainers in the entire world belong here.

Legendary

The highest and rarest tier. Legendary-level Trainers and Pokémon have surpassed what is normally considered achievable, possessing unparalleled abilities, accomplishments, and reputations that set them apart from even Champions. Legendary-level Pokémon may possess power comparable to the world’s strongest, well, Legendary Pokémon, while a Legendary Trainer may be someone whose feats have become the stuff of history and myth. Fabled individuals fall under this tier, those only spoken of in legendary tales and stories.

Pokémon Tier Differences

Stellara uses a simple system to determine the difference in strength between Pokémon based on their Tiers. The greater the difference, the more difficult it becomes for the weaker Pokémon to compete.

  • Same Tier: Pokémon are considered relatively equal in overall strength and capabilities. However, species, typing, abilities and strategy may still influence the outcome of a battle.
  • One Tier Apart: The stronger Pokémon has a clear advantage, but the weaker Pokémon can still win through effective strategy, favorable circumstances or superior battle performance.
  • Two Tiers Apart: The stronger Pokémon significantly outclasses the weaker Pokémon. Winning is extremely difficult and generally requires exceptional strategy, favorable circumstances or the stronger Pokémon being substantially weakened.
  • Three or More Tiers Apart: The stronger Pokémon overwhelmingly outclasses the weaker Pokémon. Under normal circumstances, the weaker Pokémon cannot reasonably win a direct battle against an opponent fighting at full strength. Victory would require extraordinary circumstances that convincingly overcome the difference in power.

Tier differences are intended to establish reasonable expectations rather than guarantee specific outcomes. Trainers should consider their Pokémon's condition, abilities, typing, environment and battle strategies while respecting the advantages established by each Tier.
