Catching Pokémon is handled so that players can obtain Pokémon with as little staff moderation as possible. Each location has a list of wild Pokémon that can be found there. Use these lists to determine which Pokémon you may encounter. The tier of wild Pokémon depends on your Trainer's tier, with a chance of encountering Pokémon that are either stronger or weaker than your Trainer. Catching a Pokémon that is Elite-tier or higher requires explicit staff approval.

IT IS ASSUMED THAT YOU ALWAYS HAVE POKÉ BALLS AVAILABLE; YOU DO NOT NEED TO PURCHASE THEM FROM THE POKÉMART. You may use any type of Poké Ball except for Master Balls.

Wild Pokémon Encounters

Whenever you want to catch a Pokémon, post in the MOD REQUEST thread with a link to the thread where you intend to catch it. Staff will generate a wild Pokémon for you based on your Trainer's progression and the location's available Pokémon.

There are two methods of encountering wild Pokémon:

RANDOM ENCOUNTERS: Staff will randomly generate a Pokémon from the location's encounter list. You CANNOT request a specific Pokémon or type through this method. If you do not want the Pokémon that was generated, you may pass on it and request another encounter. You may pass up to three times per catching thread. After your third pass, you must either proceed with the final encounter or end your catching attempt.

POKÉPASS ENCOUNTERS: Poképasses allow you to guarantee an encounter with either a specific Pokémon type or species. To use a Poképass, state that you are using one in your Mod Request and specify the type or Pokémon you want. Requested Pokémon must still follow the location's encounter restrictions and your Trainer's progression. Poképasses do not guarantee a particular tier, and evolved Pokémon may be restricted if they are too powerful for your Trainer.

Wild Pokémon Tiers

Wild Pokémon tiers are randomly generated based on your Trainer's current tier. Generally, wild Pokémon will be around the same tier as your Trainer, although stronger and weaker Pokémon may also appear.

The following probabilities are used when generating wild Pokémon:

  • 55%: Same tier as your Trainer.
  • 20%: One tier above your Trainer.
  • 20%: One tier below your Trainer.
  • 5%: Any lower eligible tier.

Wild Pokémon encountered through standard catching methods cannot exceed Ultra-tier. If your Trainer is Ultra-tier or higher, any probability that would generate an ineligible higher-tier Pokémon will be redistributed among eligible tiers. The same applies when your Trainer is too low-tier to encounter weaker Pokémon.

Elite-tier Pokémon and higher require staff approval before they can be captured, regardless of your Trainer's tier or whether you are using a Poképass.

Catching Process

Once staff generates your encounter, you are free to roleplay the catching process without further staff moderation. You may decide the Pokémon's gender, personality, standard Ability, and moves, provided everything follows the established rules. Hidden Abilities and other restricted characteristics must follow their respective requirements.

You may catch Pokémon through traditional battles, befriending them, earning their trust, or any other believable method. There is no minimum word count or number of posts required for a capture, but you are expected to put genuine effort into the encounter. Simply encountering a Pokémon and immediately declaring it caught without meaningful interaction is not acceptable.

Make sure to portray the Pokémon's personality and behavior consistently throughout the encounter. You are free to create its personality yourself, and there is no longer a minimum word count for doing so.

Standard captures do not require staff approval once the encounter has been generated. After successfully catching a Pokémon, you may add it to your Pokémon PC along with its information and a link to the completed catching thread. Staff reserve the right to review captures and request corrections if any rules have been violated.

Collaborative Catching

Players are encouraged to catch Pokémon together! Multiple Trainers may attempt to catch Pokémon in the same thread, and each Trainer may have their own encounter generated by staff. You may also request multiple encounters at once by including the participating Trainers and their encounter requests in the Mod Request thread.

Players are allowed and encouraged to portray wild Pokémon for one another. For example, one player may portray the Pokémon that another Trainer is attempting to catch, determining its personality, actions, and reactions throughout the encounter. Players may also portray their own wild Pokémon if they prefer.

You are free to develop catching plots together, whether that involves battling wild Pokémon, exploring dangerous areas, helping one another, or building friendships with the Pokémon you encounter. Just make sure everyone involved agrees on how the roleplay will proceed. Do not control another player's character or deliberately prevent them from completing their capture without their agreement.

Each generated Pokémon belongs to the encounter of the Trainer who requested it. Other Trainers may assist with the capture, but they cannot claim that Pokémon for themselves without staff approval.

Catching Pokémon is also permitted in regular roleplay threads. You do not need to create a thread specifically for catching Pokémon. If you decide to catch a Pokémon during an ongoing adventure, simply request an encounter through the normal process.

If another player becomes inactive or leaves a catching thread, you may continue the encounter yourself or have another player take over portraying the wild Pokémon.

Catching Limits

You are allowed TWO SUCCESSFUL CAPTURES PER WEEK, regardless of whether they occur in solo or collaborative threads. The week resets every Monday at Central Time, and captures count toward the week in which they are completed.

You may participate in up to two active threads in which you are attempting to catch Pokémon. However, helping another Trainer with their capture or portraying a wild Pokémon for someone else does NOT count toward this limit.

Unsuccessful encounters, abandoned catching attempts, and helping other players do not consume your weekly capture allowance.

Remember that staff members reserve the right to intervene in any catching thread if necessary, especially when rules are violated or special encounters are involved. Otherwise, players are encouraged to handle their catching plots independently and enjoy the freedom of creating their own stories!
