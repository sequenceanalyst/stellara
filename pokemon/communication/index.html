Pokémon can understand human language, although humans do not necessarily understand Pokémon in return. On Stellara, players may decide how well their characters understand Pokémon. Some characters may understand only their own Pokémon, others may understand all Pokémon, and some may not understand Pokémon speech at all.

When Pokémon speak, their dialogue should be written in parentheses to indicate that they are speaking in Pokéspeak rather than human language. The dialogue inside the parentheses represents a translation of what the Pokémon is saying.

( Hey! I'm Pikachu! )

( How come I go back to level 5 every single time we go to a new region, Ash? )

( In fact, why the hell are you still ten years old even though Pokemon has been going on for twenty years? )

Certain Pokémon can communicate directly in human language, either through speech or telepathy. Psychic-type Pokémon may possess telepathic abilities, while some Pokémon of other types may also be capable of human speech or telepathic communication.

Understanding Pokéspeak does not automatically grant a character telepathy, mind-reading or other supernatural abilities. If you are uncertain whether or not a Pokemon can speak the human language, refer to this page HERE. If you still have questions, ask a member of staff.
