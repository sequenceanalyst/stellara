Overview

Team Crux is one of the most notorious criminal organizations operating within the Stellara Region. Originally established as a small gang, Team Crux has grown into an extensive organization with considerable wealth, influence and resources. Its members are feared throughout Stellara, and the presence of Team Crux agents is often enough to cause widespread panic.

Team Crux seeks to establish control over the Stellara Region, including gaining influence over its Pokémon League and other major institutions. The organization is also heavily involved in scientific research aimed at unlocking the full potential of Pokémon and applying their extraordinary capabilities to humans.

Much of this research involves morphs, humans who possess Pokémon DNA and may exhibit characteristics or abilities associated with Pokémon. Team Crux is particularly interested in developing increasingly powerful morphs, and rumors suggest that the organization hopes to create an army of enhanced humans and Pokémon. Some even believe that Team Crux intends to harness the powers of Legendary Pokémon for its own purposes.

To accomplish its goals, Team Crux engages in numerous criminal activities, including theft, smuggling, kidnapping, illegal experimentation and murder. The organization possesses little regard for those who stand in its way and is willing to use extreme measures to protect its interests.

Despite its brutality, Team Crux is known for treating its own members exceptionally well. The organization provides its members with valuable resources, opportunities and support, fostering considerable loyalty among its ranks. However, members are still expected to follow orders, fulfill their responsibilities and remain loyal to the organization.

Joining Team Crux

Characters may join Team Crux by seeking out its members, receiving an invitation or having established connections to the organization. Most applicants must prove their value through an initiation task, battle or other demonstration of their capabilities before being accepted.

Team Crux may also recruit individuals through intimidation, coercion or kidnapping. However, forcing another player's character to join the organization requires that player's agreement and must follow Stellara's established roleplay rules.

Member Benefits

Team Crux members receive several benefits:

  • Exclusive Missions: Access to Team Crux Missions that may offer special rewards and opportunities unavailable through ordinary Missions.
  • Transportation and Facilities: Access to Team Crux's transportation network and bases throughout Stellara, subject to location restrictions and narrative circumstances.
  • Special Resources: Access to equipment, research facilities and other resources that may become available as the organization's storyline progresses.

Additional benefits may become available as members advance through Team Crux. However, membership does not automatically grant access to restricted Pokémon, experimental enhancements or other abilities that would normally require approval. All benefits remain subject to Stellara's established rules.

Ranks

Team Crux operates through a strict hierarchy consisting of five ranks.

AGENT is the lowest rank in Team Crux and makes up the majority of its membership. Agents perform most of the organization's field operations, including gathering intelligence, acquiring resources, completing Missions and carrying out orders from their superiors. Although their authority is limited, Agents are considered valuable members of the organization.

OFFICER is the next rank above Agent. Officers are responsible for leading squads of Agents, coordinating operations and ensuring that assignments are completed successfully. They also serve as the primary point of contact for Agents under their command. Becoming an Officer requires demonstrated experience, reliability and the ability to lead others effectively.

ENFORCER is one of Team Crux's upper ranks. Enforcers oversee multiple Officers and their assigned Agents, coordinate larger operations and manage local Team Crux bases. They may also develop Missions and assign responsibilities to lower-ranking members. Enforcers have access to more exclusive resources and are expected to demonstrate considerable judgment, leadership and loyalty.

DIRECTOR is the highest rank beneath the Leader. Directors oversee major divisions or territories within Stellara and are responsible for coordinating large portions of Team Crux's operations. They possess substantial authority over the organization's members and resources. Only a small number of Directors exist at any given time, and reaching this position requires exceptional accomplishments and the confidence of Team Crux's leadership.

The LEADER is the highest-ranking member of Team Crux and is responsible for directing the organization's overall ambitions and operations. The Leader possesses the greatest authority within Team Crux, although maintaining control over such a powerful organization requires the continued loyalty and cooperation of its senior members. This position is currently occupied and cannot ordinarily be obtained through promotions.

Promotions

Promotions are handled on a case-by-case basis and must be approved by staff. Members who believe they have earned a promotion may submit a request in the Team Crux Roster thread, including links to relevant roleplay threads and accomplishments.

Staff will consider the character's successful operations, leadership, judgment, loyalty and overall contributions to Team Crux. Completing Missions or possessing powerful Pokémon does not automatically guarantee a promotion.

Higher ranks carry greater authority and responsibility, and promotions become increasingly difficult as members advance. Members seeking positions such as Enforcer or Director should demonstrate that they are capable of managing other members, organizing operations and contributing meaningfully to the organization's goals.

Team Crux members are expected to respect the organization's hierarchy while continuing to follow Stellara's general roleplay rules. Membership does not permit players to force outcomes upon other participants or disregard established restrictions.
