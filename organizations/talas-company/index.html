Headquartered in Eridani City, Talas Company is one of Stellara's largest energy corporations. Once celebrated for its contributions to the region's industrial development, the company became embroiled in controversy following the infamous Talas Incident, which exposed widespread mistreatment of Electric-type Pokémon at one of its power plants. Although Talas has since implemented reforms and invested heavily in rebuilding its reputation, internal disagreements over the company's future continue to create tension between executives advocating for change and those determined to protect its commercial interests.
