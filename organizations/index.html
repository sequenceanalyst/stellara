Stellara is home to numerous organizations, corporations, and institutions that play important roles in the region's development and everyday life. From the prestigious Pokémon League to major corporations and criminal organizations, these groups influence Stellara in a variety of ways. Some offer opportunities for aspiring Trainers and professionals, while others operate under more questionable circumstances.

Team Crux in brief

Team Crux is a notorious criminal organization operating throughout Stellara. Known for its involvement in illegal activities and dangerous scientific experimentation, the organization seeks to expand its influence while pursuing ambitions that frequently place it at odds with regional authorities. Despite ongoing efforts to combat its operations, Team Crux remains a persistent threat, attracting individuals motivated by wealth, power, ambition, or their own personal convictions.
