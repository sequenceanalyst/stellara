Purchase Process

When you are ready to make a purchase, make a thread in this board and list everything that you want to buy. A staff member will then check the thread and approve whatever purchases you made. Every time you want to make another purchase, you will post in the same thread you made before; do not create a new thread for every single time you want to buy something. When you are ready for a staff member to check your shop thread, post in the MOD REQUEST section of the Discord.

Pokémon Tier Advancement

Trainers can spend EXP to advance their Pokémon to higher tiers. Each tier has a specific EXP requirement, which must be paid before the Pokémon can advance.

  • Rookie50EXP
  • Experienced100EXP
  • Great200EXP
  • Ultra300EXP
  • Elite400EXP
  • Champion500EXP
  • Master750EXP
  • Legendary1,000EXP

Pokémon must progress through the tiers in order and may only advance one tier per week. Additionally, Pokémon cannot advance beyond the tier permitted by their Trainer's progression restrictions.

When requesting a tier advancement, specify the Pokémon's name, its current tier, and the tier it is advancing to. Be sure to follow all applicable Pokémon Progression rules.

Catching

Trainers can request random wild Pokémon encounters without spending EXP. However, those who wish to encounter Pokémon of a specific type or species may purchase Poképasses.

  • Type PoképassAllows you to guarantee an encounter with a Pokémon of a specific elemental type. The Pokémon is randomly generated from the species available at your chosen location.25EXP
  • Species PoképassAllows you to guarantee an encounter with a specific Pokémon species, provided that it is available at your chosen location and meets your Trainer's progression restrictions.75EXP

Poképasses do not guarantee a Pokémon's tier or a successful capture. All encounters must follow Stellara's Catching Rules, including weekly capture limits and restrictions on rare or exceptionally powerful Pokémon. Poképasses are consumed upon use.

Battle Upgrades

  • Power CandyAllows a Pokémon to increase the number of moves it can know from eight to ten. This upgrade can only be applied once per Pokémon. If you wish to increase the move limit of multiple Pokémon, you must purchase a Power Candy for each one.100EXP
  • Ability CapsuleAllows you to change a Pokémon's Ability, including changing it to its Hidden Ability if the species possesses one. Each purchase applies to one Pokémon50EXP
  • Shiny CharmAllows you to turn a Pokémon you own, or a wild Pokémon you encounter, into a Shiny Pokémon. Each Shiny Charm can only be used once, meaning you must purchase additional charms to make multiple Pokémon Shiny.500EXP
  • Mega AccessoryA special accessory containing a Key Stone that allows a Trainer's Pokémon to Mega Evolve. The accessory may take the form of a bracelet, necklace, amulet, or another suitable item. A corresponding Mega Stone is also required for the Pokémon to Mega Evolve. Mega Stones cannot be purchased from the Pokémart and must instead be obtained through missions.200EXP
  • Z-RingA special bracelet that allows Trainers to harness Z-Power and use Z-Moves. A corresponding Z-Crystal is required.125EXP
  • Z-CrystalA crystal that allows a Pokémon to use a corresponding Z-Move when paired with a Z-Ring. Each Z-Crystal must be purchased separately.100EXP
  • Dynamax BandA special device that allows Trainers to Dynamax or Gigantamax their Pokémon at designated Power Spots.200EXP
  • Max HoneyA special kind of honey that allows a compatible Pokémon to unlock its Gigantamax form upon consumption. A Dynamax Band and access to a Power Spot are still required to Gigantamax.150EXP
  • Tera OrbA special device that allows Trainers to Terastallize their Pokémon. Purchasing a Tera Orb includes one complimentary Tera Shard for a Pokémon and Tera Type of your choice.200EXP
  • Tera ShardA crystal attuned to a specific Pokémon that allows it to Terastallize into a particular Tera Type. Each Tera Shard is assigned to one Pokémon and one type. Pokémon may possess multiple Tera Shards, but each additional shard must be purchased separately.100EXP

All special battle transformations must follow their respective requirements and restrictions, regardless of whether the necessary equipment has been purchased.

Medicine

Trainers can only have THREE Potions and ONE of every other Potion in their inventory at a time.

  • PotionHeals minor injuries such as small bruises, small cuts and other light wounds on a Pokemon.50XP
  • Super PotionHeals standard injuries such as bruises, cuts and other moderate wounds on a Pokemon.75XP
  • Hyper PotionHeals serious injuries such as deep bruises, deep cuts and other severe wounds a Pokemon.100XP
  • Max PotionCompletely heals any and all injuries on a Pokemon.150XP
  • Full RestoreCompletely heals any and all injuries on a Pokemon while also curing it of any status ailments.200XP

Evolution Items

Evolutionary Stones and other evolution items allow eligible Pokémon to evolve through their respective methods.

  • Evolutionary Stone50EXP
  • Other Evolution Item50EXP
  • Friendship Evolution50EXP
  • Trade Evolution Service50EXP

Friendship Evolutions may be purchased for Pokémon that ordinarily evolve through friendship, provided they are at least Experienced Tier unless otherwise specified.

Trade Evolutions may be completed through an actual player trade or by purchasing the Pokémart's Trade Evolution service. Pokémon that require a held item must possess that item before evolving.

Purchasing an evolution item or service does not increase a Pokémon's Tier or bypass applicable evolutionary requirements.
