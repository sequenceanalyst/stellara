The Stellara Pokémon Exchange is an organization that specializes in the adoption, sale and trading of Pokémon. With facilities throughout Stellara, the Exchange allows Trainers to acquire new Pokémon or find new homes for Pokémon they no longer wish to keep. All Pokémon available through the Exchange are evaluated to ensure that they are healthy and suitable for adoption.

The Exchange offers three primary services:

Adoption

Trainers may purchase Pokémon from the Exchange's available listings. Prices vary depending on species, rarity, Tier and other characteristics.

Available Pokémon

Choose a Pokémon to see its full details. Listings change over time, so check back often.

Selling

Trainers may submit Pokémon for evaluation and receive an offer in EXP. Trainers may accept or decline the offer.

Appraisals

Trainers may present a Pokémon for evaluation and receive potential trade offers, which may include Pokémon of comparable value or other desirable characteristics. Trainers may accept an offer or reject them all.

Restrictions

Available Pokémon, prices and trade offers may change over time. Not every species will necessarily be available, and appraisals do not guarantee that a particular Pokémon will be offered.

All Pokémon obtained through the Exchange must follow Stellara's established ownership, acquisition and progression restrictions. Legendary Pokémon, Mythical Pokémon and other restricted Pokémon cannot ordinarily be obtained through the Exchange. The Exchange may also reject transactions involving Pokémon obtained illegally or under questionable circumstances.

Players may not repeatedly capture and sell Pokémon for the sole purpose of generating excessive amounts of EXP. Staff may limit or reject transactions that attempt to exploit the system.

Pokémon obtained through the Exchange retain their established personalities, experiences and characteristics. Purchasing or trading for a Pokémon does not guarantee its immediate trust or obedience, and additional roleplay may be necessary to establish a relationship with its new Trainer.
