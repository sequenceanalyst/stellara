Services

The Black Market is an underground network involved in the illegal sale, purchase and trading of Pokémon. Unlike the Stellara Pokémon Exchange, the Black Market operates without official regulation and frequently deals in Pokémon that are rare, unusually valuable or difficult to obtain through legitimate means.

The Black Market provides the same general services as the Exchange, including purchasing, selling and appraisals. However, its listings may include rarer Pokémon, unusual characteristics and more valuable trade offers. Prices and availability vary, and particularly rare Pokémon may only become available through special events or staff-approved storylines.

Restrictions

Pokémon obtained through the Black Market must still follow Stellara's ownership, acquisition and progression restrictions. Legendary Pokémon, Mythical Pokémon and other restricted Pokémon are not automatically available, and exceptional transactions may require staff approval. Players cannot use the Black Market to bypass established rules.

Narrative Risks

Unlike the Exchange, the Black Market does not guarantee that Pokémon have been obtained legally or treated appropriately. Certain transactions may carry narrative consequences, including:

  • Stolen Pokémon: Previous owners may attempt to recover their Pokémon, potentially involving authorities or other organizations.
  • Mistreatment: Pokémon may have experienced neglect or abuse, making them distrustful of their new Trainers or requiring additional care.
  • Counterfeit Documentation: Falsified ownership records may create complications if a Pokémon's origins are investigated.
  • Criminal Attention: Valuable purchases may attract criminals seeking to steal Pokémon, reclaim them or pressure their owners.
  • Outstanding Obligations: Dealers may offer valuable Pokémon in exchange for favors, services or other commitments.
  • Investigations: Illegal transactions may attract the attention of law enforcement, Rangers or other organizations.

These consequences are intended to create roleplay opportunities rather than automatically punish players. They are not guaranteed with every transaction and should remain appropriate to the Pokémon and circumstances involved.

Players should generally be informed when a transaction carries significant narrative risks, although the exact consequences may not always be revealed beforehand. Particularly consequential transactions should be discussed with staff or other involved participants.

The Black Market may offer greater opportunities than the Exchange, but Trainers must be prepared to accept the risks associated with dealing in the underground Pokémon trade.
